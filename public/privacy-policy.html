Privacy Policy for The Juice App

Effective Date: 08/12/2025

The Juice App is an anonymous, men-only social platform for sharing stories, comments, and advice—without judgment. This policy explains in plain English how we handle your information.

Information We Collect

  • Account info: username and email to create and manage your account.
  • Content you create: stories, comments, and reactions (with basic timestamps or tags).
  • Device info: device model, OS version, app version, and basic diagnostics to keep the app working.

We collect only what’s needed to run the app. We do not sell your data and we do not use third‑party ad SDKs or track you across other apps and websites.

How We Use Your Information

  • Operate the app: create accounts, enable posting and interactions, and show relevant content.
  • Safety and moderation: maintain community standards and prevent abuse or fraud.
  • Improve reliability: fix bugs, measure performance, and ship updates.
  • Communicate with you: send essential service messages (e.g., security or account changes).

Sharing Your Information

  • Service providers: trusted vendors (e.g., hosting, database, email) who help us operate the app. They may use data only to provide these services to us.
  • Legal and safety: when required by law or necessary to protect users, our rights, or the platform.

We do not sell personal data or share it for advertising or cross‑app tracking.

Data Security

We use safeguards like encryption in transit, access controls, and monitoring. No method is 100% secure, but we work to protect your information and limit what we collect. We keep data only as long as needed for the purposes above or as required by law.

Your Choices

  • Access, update, or delete your data by contacting us. You may also request account deletion.
  • Emails: you can opt out of non‑essential messages.
  • Anonymity: avoid including personal details in posts or comments if you wish to stay anonymous.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy. We’ll revise the “Effective Date” above and notify you in the app or by email when changes are significant.
