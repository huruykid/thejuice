Privacy Policy for thejuice

Effective date: August 9, 2025

Overview

thejuice ("we", "us", or "our") is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have. By using the app or website, you agree to this policy.

Information We Collect

  • Account data: email, username, profile details.
  • Verification data: selfie images you upload for account verification.
  • Location data: approximate city/location when you allow location access.
  • Usage data: app interactions, device info, and diagnostics.
  • Content: stories, comments, reactions and related metadata you create.

We use Supabase for authentication, database, and storage services.

Device Permissions

  • Camera: used only to capture a verification selfie and optional profile photos.
  • Photos: optional, to select an image from your library.
  • Location: optional, to show nearby content and improve discovery.

You can change these permissions anytime in your device settings.

App Privacy Details (Apple)

This summary reflects how thejuice handles data as disclosed in App Store Connect.

  • Data linked to you: Contact info (email), Identifiers (user ID), User content (selfies, stories, comments), Usage data (app interactions), Approximate location (when enabled).
  • Data used for tracking: None. We do not track you across apps or websites or use third‑party ad SDKs.
  • Data sharing: We share with service providers only (e.g., Supabase) to operate the service; we do not sell personal data.
  • Data retention: Kept only as long as needed for the purposes described below or as required by law.
  • Deletion: You can delete your account in the app (Privacy Settings → Delete Account) or email support@teaappformen.com.

How We Use Information

  • Provide and improve core features, including discovery and moderation.
  • Verify accounts and maintain community safety.
  • Communicate with you about updates, security, and support.
  • Prevent fraud, abuse, and violations of our Terms.

How We Share Information

  • Service providers: e.g., Supabase and email providers to run the service.
  • Legal and safety: when required by law or to protect our users and platform.

We do not sell your personal information.

Data Retention

We retain information only as long as necessary for the purposes described here, unless a longer period is required by law.

Your Choices & Rights

  • Access, correct, or delete your data: use in‑app Privacy Settings or contact Support.
  • Withdraw consent for optional permissions (camera, location, photos) in device settings.
  • Opt out of certain communications using in‑app controls or unsubscribe links.

Security

We implement technical and organizational measures to protect data, including encryption in transit and restricted access. No system is 100% secure, but we continuously improve our safeguards.

Children's Privacy

thejuice is intended for users 13+ and not directed to children. If you believe a child provided personal information, contact us to remove it.

International Transfers

Your data may be processed in regions where our providers operate. We rely on appropriate safeguards as applicable.

Updates to This Policy

We may update this policy. We will change the "Effective date" above and, when appropriate, notify you in the app or by email.
