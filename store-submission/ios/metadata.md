## Description (1365/4000)
Juice is the Tea app, but for men. Verified men share what they really experienced with the women they've dated — one green or red flag, plus the story — so you can look her up before your next date.

EVERY MEMBER IS HUMAN-VERIFIED
No bots, no fakes, no women posing as men. Every account passes a one-time camera selfie check reviewed by a real person before it's approved. What's shared inside stays inside a verified community.

YOU POST ANONYMOUSLY
Your name is never shown. Share your experience honestly without it following you around. Google sign-in is only for login — we never post anything or reveal who you are.

GREEN FLAGS AND RED FLAGS
Reviews are simple: one flag, one story. Was she genuine? A green flag tells the next guy. Did something feel off? A red flag with your experience could save someone months.

SEARCH BEFORE YOU DATE
Type a first name and city and see what verified men have already shared. Knowledge you could never get from her profile.

BUILT WITH ACCOUNTABILITY
Every post can be reported. A moderation team reviews flagged content, and anyone who believes a post about them is unfair can request removal through our public dispute process — no account needed. Blocking, reporting, and removal tools are built in.

Juice is free to join. Verification takes about a minute.

Dating is a two-way street. Now the information is too.
